Our Story

Student Getaways started with a simple idea: college students should be able to travel and have fun without spending a ton of money. Between classes, jobs, and other responsibilities, finding time to plan a trip can be difficult. We wanted to create a business that makes traveling easier and more affordable for students.

Our goal is to help college students explore new places, make memories with friends, and take a break from their busy schedules. We organize affordable trips and help with the planning so students can focus on enjoying their experience.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I have to be a college student?

Our trips are mainly designed for college students who want to travel, have fun, and save money.

Are the trips expensive?

No! We try to keep our trips affordable so students can enjoy traveling without worrying about spending too much money.

Can I pay for my trip in installments?

Yes! Some trips allow students to pay a deposit first and pay the remaining balance before the trip.

How do I reserve a spot?

You can visit our Trips & Services page to see what trips we offer and contact us about reserving your spot.

What happens if I have questions?

You can contact our team by email or phone during business hours. We are happy to help!
